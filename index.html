A letter for you.

Dear Maria,

I know you like getting letters, so I thought I'd make you a little place where I can keep writing them for you.

Maybe it's not quite the same as getting an actual letter from me, especially with all this distance between us, but hopefully this can be my little way of making the distance feel a bit smaller.

This is just somewhere I can leave pieces of my thoughts, little memories, plans, and things I don't want to forget.

And maybe one day we'll look back at all of this and realize how many places we've been, how many things we've done and how far we've come together.

For now, I'll just keep adding things here. More letters, more plans, more memories.

Until then, I'm really looking forward to the next time I get to see you.
